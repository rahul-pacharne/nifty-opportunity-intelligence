## 14. Notification priority levels

### L1 — Early warning

Purpose: inform the user that a possible opportunity is developing.

Low urgency. Watch only. No trade recommendation. Usually sent through Telegram.

### L2 — Signal strengthening

Purpose: notify the user when an existing signal materially improves.

The existing signal is updated. No duplicate alert. Probability or confidence changes significantly. Supporting evidence is included.

### L3 — High-quality opportunity

Purpose: highlight a setup that has passed the major directional filters.

Strong confidence. Expected move identified. Options evaluation pending or partially complete. The user should pay attention.

### L4 — Trade recommendation

Purpose: deliver an actionable manual trading recommendation.

Direction confirmed. Option selected or selection criteria provided. Risk/reward, liquidity, and expected value evaluated. Manual approval required.

Delivery: Telegram, WhatsApp, and email.

### L5 — Critical update

Purpose: immediately notify the user of a material change.

Examples: signal invalidated, trade recommendation cancelled, risk conditions changed, a major overnight event, or a significant change in expected direction.

Delivery: Telegram, WhatsApp, and email.

## 15. Notification design principles

1. Notifications must be actionable and meaningful.
2. Avoid duplicate notifications.
3. Update existing signals when possible.
4. Do not notify for insignificant probability changes.
5. Clearly distinguish WATCH from TRADE RECOMMENDATION.
6. Every recommendation must include its reasoning.
7. Every recommendation must include risk information.
8. Every signal must have an expiry or invalidation condition.
9. Every signal must be tracked to its final outcome.
10. NO TRADE is a valid and successful system decision.
11. A correct market prediction must not automatically trigger a trade recommendation.
12. Option profitability must be evaluated independently of market direction.

NOI should be quiet most of the time. It should interrupt the user only when genuinely important information or a high-quality actionable opportunity is detected.

The objective is not more alerts or more trades. The objective is fewer, higher-quality opportunities where the expected reward justifies the risk.
